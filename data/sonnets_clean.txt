1

When April wakes the hawthorn into bloom,
And mildest winds unbar the frozen field,
The lark ascends above the furrowed gloom,
While shades of winter slowly quit and yield.
So from my heart your gentle presence drives
The hoarded frost that long in silence lay,
And every better hope within revives
To greet the promise of your temperate day.
Yet spring is frail, and clouds may dim the sun,
And tender petals fall before they fruit;
Still I would bless the good that has begun,
Though joy be brief and every blossom mute.
For one sweet hour, if all the year were sere,
Would teach the soul what makes the living dear.

2

The candle gutters in the scholar's room,
Yet still his hand pursues the patient line,
He wars with time, with weariness, with gloom,
To snare in words some glimmer half divine.
The midnight panes return no friendly face,
The house is hushed, the embers sink and sigh,
But thought keeps watch within that narrow place,
And feeds on stars that none but minds descry.
So labor oft must bloom where none applaud,
And truth be courted far from trumpet's breath;
Its gains are lean, its recompense seems flawed,
Its bridegroom hours are kin to little death.
Yet what is won in silence, stern and slow,
May light an age long after we lie low.

3

When envy marks the wreath another wears,
And counts his honors with a jaundiced eye,
It nurses want and fattens private cares,
Till peace grows thin and inward joys run dry.
For others' praise can neither add nor take
The secret measure of thy proper worth;
No stolen star can make thy heaven shake,
Nor borrowed dawn unmake thy native birth.
Turn then thy hand to that which thou canst tend,
And let thy craft, not coveting, be guide;
The fairest laurels from true labors bend,
And root more deep when vanity has died.
Who blesses others need not beg increase,
For generous hearts are wealthy most in peace.

4

At dusk the harbor gathers mast and sail,
And folds the sea's long glitter into gray,
The market calls grow faint, the hammers fail,
And weary men unyoke the toiling day.
One lamp by one begins along the quay,
Like patient stars set low for mortal eyes,
While from the west the last reluctant ray
Withdraws its gold and leaves the cool surprise.
So after strife the spirit seeks a shore
Where noise falls off and troubled reckonings cease;
It asks not triumph, nor abundant store,
But one sure hearth and some sufficient peace.
The richest rest is not in palaced sleep,
But where a faithful love its watch doth keep.

5

The oak was once an acorn in the grass,
A slight green thought no larger than a tear,
Yet storms have bowed and many years did pass,
And still it stands, more rooted for the year.
So virtue grows not by a single deed,
Nor wisdom by one maxim quickly learned;
They gather ring by ring from trial and need,
By losses borne and errors slowly turned.
Mock not small acts because they seem too mean
To win the shout that follows grand display;
The brook begins where little springs are seen,
Yet shapes the stone and finds at last the bay.
Great souls are made by increments unseen,
And harvest ripens long before the glean.

6

When summer fades beneath the amber sky,
And harvest bends the weary fields with gold,
The swallows wheel where cooling breezes sigh,
And whisper tales of journeys yet untold.
The orchard's fruit in quiet sweetness lies,
Awaiting hands that pluck with patient care,
While daylight dims in softly burning dyes,
And dusk lays purple fingers on the air.
So too my youth, once ripe with careless fire,
Must yield its glow to time’s deliberate art,
Yet memory guards each bright and bold desire,
And keeps their warmth alive within the heart.
Though seasons turn and fleeting hours depart,
Love’s gathered fruit outlasts the fading start.

7

In shadowed courts where silent statues stand,
And marble heroes watch the years decay,
Their frozen brows command the passing land,
Yet hear no voice that calls them back to day.
The ivy climbs in slow, encircling grace,
And claims the stones once polished bright with pride,
No trumpet stirs within that solemn place,
Nor banners in triumphant winds abide.
Thus glory built on conquest and on fear
Shall crumble like the dust it hoped to reign,
For mortal praise, though loud upon the ear,
Cannot preserve what time will not sustain.
But virtue, sown in humble, living deed,
Outlasts the sculptor’s art and tyrant’s creed.

8

When midnight draws her sable curtain tight,
And lamps grow faint against the swelling gloom,
The mind awakens to its inward sight,
And wanders freely through imagined room.
Unfettered thought ascends on unseen wings,
Beyond the bounds of iron circumstance,
And fashions worlds from small, forgotten things,
Granting the soul a sovereign expanse.
So dreams, though fragile as the morning mist,
Reveal desires daylight dares not show,
They trace the shapes by hidden longings kissed,
And plant the seeds from which our actions grow.
What first in shadowed sleep begins to rise
May bloom in truth beneath awakened skies.

9

The river moves with unresisting will,
And carves its patient path through rock and clay,
No storm can force it wholly to be still,
Nor drought persuade its current not to stray.
It bends but breaks not at the mountain’s side,
And finds through yielding strength a forward way,
Though cliffs in stubborn opposition bide,
It sings its course from dawn to closing day.
So let my heart in trial learn that art,
To flow around the hardness fate may send,
And not in brittle defiance part,
Where wiser softness might the struggle mend.
For power lies not always in the blow,
But in the grace by which we onward go.

10

If sorrow press with unrelenting hand,
And clouds obscure the promise of the sun,
Remember how the smallest grains of sand
Compose the shore where mighty seas are run.
No grief so vast but time will wear it down,
As waves smooth jagged stone to gentle form,
Nor night so deep it claims the lasting crown,
For dawn returns though long endured the storm.
Hold fast to hope though trembling be thy breath,
And trust the quiet labor of the years,
For life persists beyond the threat of death,
And dries at last the bitterest of tears.
What seems an end in anguish sharply drawn
May prove the gate through which new strength is born.

11

When autumn’s breath upon the forest falls,
And crimson crowns the once-emerald trees,
A golden hush along the valley calls,
And bends the wheat in slow, consenting seas.
The orchards bow with sweet and ripened weight,
While daylight shortens like a measured sigh,
The sun descends in contemplative state,
And paints its farewell softly on the sky.
So too the heart that once in summer burned,
Must learn to glow with quieter, deeper flame,
For passions fierce to tempered warmth are turned,
Yet lose not strength though altered be their name.
In mellow light a richer truth is shown:
What fades in fire in embers may be grown.

12

The moon ascends upon a silver thread,
And bathes the restless town in borrowed gleam,
Soft shadows where the weary labor tread
Are gentled by her pale, forgiving beam.
The rooftops shimmer like a muted tide,
And windows glow as stars in ordered line,
While secrets in the silent streets abide,
Untroubled by the clang of noon’s design.
Thus night reveals what daylight will not show,
The tender doubts that hide in private breast,
And thoughts that in the crowd refuse to grow,
Yet bloom in dark when souls are put to rest.
In quiet hours the truest voices rise,
Unmasked beneath the watch of patient skies.

13

A tempest gathers on the distant plain,
And bends the grasses low before its might,
The thunder speaks in resonant refrain,
And cleaves the trembling firmament with light.
The rain descends in fierce, unyielding streams,
And floods the paths where dust had long been king,
Yet from the storm awaken buried dreams,
And thirsty roots drink deep from suffering.
So trials, though they shake the steadfast frame,
May cleanse the heart of idle vanity,
And through their roar refine the inner flame,
Till strength is forged in chosen constancy.
What storms unmake in pride and shallow art,
They build in courage at the spirit’s heart.

14

The candle burns with steady, modest glow,
And spends itself to guard the dark at bay,
Its wax descends in quiet ebb and flow,
While fragile light holds watch till break of day.
No trumpet marks its sacrificial part,
Nor crowd attends its vigil through the night,
Yet faithful flame performs a noble art,
In small persistence lies its truest might.
So let my deeds, though humble in their span,
Cast warmth upon the paths where others tread,
For greatness dwells not always in the plan,
But in the gentle word and daily bread.
A life that gives though unobserved by fame
May shine more bright than any lauded name.

15

When envy’s seed within the breast is sown,
It sprouts in shadow, pale and twisting fast,
And chokes the joy that might have freely grown,
By clinging fierce to grievances long past.
It measures worth by others’ passing gain,
And drinks from wells that never satisfy,
Till bitterness becomes a tightening chain,
And dims the hopeful prospect of the eye.
Yet gratitude uproots that creeping vine,
And clears the soil where peace may flourish fair,
It turns the gaze from borrowed, false design,
To gifts already resting in one’s care.
Contented hearts need not another’s throne,
For riches bloom where thankful seeds are sown.

16

The scholar bends above his weathered page,
And traces lines by distant sages writ,
He seeks to bind within a narrow cage
The boundless truths that time refuses fit.
Yet knowledge grows not solely from the pen,
Nor wisdom dwell in ink alone confined,
For living breath and fellowship with men
Refine the rough conclusions of the mind.
Let study join with kindness hand in hand,
And learning serve the common, mortal need,
For sterile thought like root in barren sand
Bears little fruit though planted with a creed.
True insight flowers where compassion leads,
And thought is wed to generous deeds.

17

The mountain stands against the shifting air,
Unmoved by whispers of the passing breeze,
Its ancient face inscribed with patient care,
A monument to silent histories.
The snows that crown its steadfast, rugged height
Melt slowly in the warmth of summer’s ray,
Yet still it keeps its posture in the light,
Though ages grind its granite bones away.
So let my purpose, firm though weather worn,
Endure the doubts that buffet from below,
For constancy through countless trials borne
Outlasts the fervor of a fleeting show.
Though time erode the surface of resolve,
A rooted will no tempest can dissolve.

18

The lark ascends upon the morning air,
And spills its song across the waking field,
No rival voice disputes its presence there,
Nor hidden bargain shapes what it must yield.
It sings because its breast is filled with tone,
And joy compels the music to be freed,
Unmindful whether praise be ever shown,
Or whether any heed its simple creed.
Thus art is pure when born of inward fire,
And not from thirst for fleeting human gaze,
For hearts respond to honest, clear desire,
More than to skill adorned with studied praise.
Let song arise where truth and gladness meet,
And find in humble flight its meaning sweet.

19

When memory stirs the ashes of the past,
And paints with longing scenes long overthrown,
The mind may wander where its shadows cast
A tender glow on joys no longer known.
Yet time demands we dwell not wholly there,
Nor build our home in yesterday’s embrace,
For life unfolds in present, breathing air,
And calls us forth to meet its living face.
Let recollection serve as guiding star,
Not anchor dragging hope beneath the wave,
For though it shines from distance faint and far,
It was not meant the present to enslave.
In balanced gaze both past and now agree,
To shape a wiser, forward constancy.

20

If love be fire that neither fades nor sleeps,
It warms the coldest chambers of the soul,
Through darkest nights its faithful vigil keeps,
And binds the fractured heart to wholeness whole.
It does not boast in loud, triumphant cry,
Nor seek applause from fickle, passing throng,
But proves its strength when trials draweth nigh,
And stands unshaken when the world goes wrong.
Through changing years and fortune’s altered face,
Its steady pulse no circumstance can sever,
For rooted deep in sacrificial grace,
It weathers all and shall endure forever.
Let lesser flames in restless winds depart,
True love burns constant at the center heart.

21

When frost adorns the silent windowpane,
And etches lace where summer once had shone,
The hearth within defies the outer rain,
And claims a warmth the bitter winds disown.
The logs in measured crackling discourse speak,
And cast their amber counsel on the wall,
While shadows shift in patterns faint and meek,
Responsive to the fire’s persuasive call.
So in adversity the inward spark
May blaze more bright when tempests rage without,
For hardship frames the light against the dark,
And steels the will against encroaching doubt.
What cold would quench in careless, softer days,
Grows strong when tried by winter’s testing blaze.

22

The market hums beneath the noon’s command,
With clamor sharp as coin on stone is thrown,
Ambition walks with ledger close at hand,
And weighs each breath in profits yet unknown.
The banners flap above the restless street,
And promises like gaudy wares are cried,
While hurried feet in anxious rhythm beat,
And fortunes turn with every shifting tide.
Yet value dwells not solely in the scale,
Nor worth confined to what the purse may claim,
For quiet gifts that never stand for sale
Outlast the glitter of a purchased name.
Let gain be guided by a nobler art,
And commerce tempered by a mindful heart.

23

A child pursues the echo of his laugh,
Untroubled by the boundaries of the hour,
He builds a kingdom from a broken staff,
And crowns himself with leaves in budding bower.
No fear of failure dims his eager stride,
Nor memory of loss restrains his play,
Imagination stands at his side,
And turns the smallest stone to sovereign sway.
Would that the grown might keep such daring sight,
And not exchange bold wonder for control,
For in that fearless trust resides a light
That guards the freshness of the aging soul.
Let wisdom join but never quite replace
The courage born of childhood’s open face.

24

The clock advances with impartial hand,
Unmoved by plea or passionate protest,
Its steady tick across the ordered land
Declares no hour more favored than the rest.
It grants alike to tyrant and to sage
The measured sum of moments in a day,
And writes upon each mortal’s fleeting page
A total none may bargain to delay.
So spend thy portion with deliberate care,
And squander not on trifles poorly weighed,
For what is lost no gold can then repair,
Nor pleading word persuade what must be paid.
In mindful use of every passing breath
Resides the art that conquers wasting death.

25

The rose unfolds beneath the patient sun,
And yields its fragrance freely to the air,
No audience required nor contest won,
Its beauty thrives though none its bloom declare.
The bee attends with low industrious hymn,
And gathers sweetness for another’s need,
While petals at the edge grow faint and dim,
Resigned to time’s unhurried quiet speed.
Thus service done without a watchful eye
Contains a dignity no praise can grant,
For deeds that bloom though recognition die
Are rooted deep where shallow honors can’t.
Let virtue flower though no herald sing,
For worth resides within the offered thing.

26

When rumor spreads on eager careless tongue,
It gathers weight with each untested claim,
And like a spark through brittle grasses flung
Consumes a field with unexamined flame.
The whispered word becomes a sharpened blade,
That wounds unseen yet leaves a lasting scar,
Till trust in cautious silence must be laid,
And friendships wander distant as a star.
Let speech be bridled by discerning mind,
And truth examined ere it be proclaimed,
For reckless talk leaves consequence behind,
And innocent names unjustly shamed.
In tempered words lies honor’s quiet guard,
And peace preserved where slander would strike hard.

27

The traveler sets his course by distant light,
Though miles of shadow stretch between his feet,
He trusts the faint but faithful gleam in sight,
And counts each weary step a small defeat.
The road may twist through valleys steep and bare,
And hunger press where comfort once was known,
Yet hope persists in thin and bracing air,
And strengthens hearts long tested and alone.
So let the goal though distant as the dawn
Direct the stride when present ground is rough,
For many noble ends are slowly drawn,
And worth the cost though passage prove enough.
By steady gaze on promise yet unseen
The far horizon narrows in between.

28

The painter lifts his brush with thoughtful pause,
And studies light that shifts upon the wall,
He knows that nature yields to hidden laws,
Yet seeks to capture more than what befall.
A stroke too bold may mar the tender hue,
A timid line leave vision incomplete,
Thus judgment guides what skill alone can do,
And patient hands pursue a balance meet.
So craft in any noble human art
Requires a marriage of the heart and mind,
For passion without measure falls apart,
And reason cold leaves living fire behind.
Where discipline and ardor intertwine,
The work achieves a harmony divine.

29

When doubt invades with subtle creeping tread,
And questions whisper what the heart once knew,
It paints with ashen shades what hope had spread,
And dims the vibrant promise once in view.
Yet scrutiny though sharp in searching aim
May cleanse belief of careless idle trust,
And purge from faith the dross of hollow claim,
Till what remains is gold refined from dust.
Let doubt not reign as tyrant of the mind,
Nor banish all conviction from its seat,
But serve as steward stern yet still confined,
That truth more sound and tested may be meet.
In balanced thought where challenge shapes belief,
The spirit finds both firmness and relief.

30

The harbor waits with arms of curving stone,
And gathers ships returned from storm and spray,
Their battered sails and weathered hulls are shown,
Yet safe at last within the sheltered bay.
The ropes are cast and weary crews descend,
With tales of peril borne on salted breath,
Each voyage marked by loss and faithful friend,
And narrow passage close to grasp of death.
So home becomes the anchor of the soul,
A steadfast quay against the tossing sea,
Where fractured parts are gently made more whole,
And restless hearts regain their constancy.
In welcome warmth where wandering journeys cease,
The tempest’s roar gives way to healing peace.

31

The lantern swings against the evening breeze,
And casts its circle on the cobbled way,
A modest star among the darkened trees,
That guards the traveler till the break of day.
Though small its flame against the vault of night,
It does not waver from its chosen ground,
But keeps a faithful watch with steady light,
Where larger fires are nowhere to be found.
So let my words, though quiet in their sound,
Give guidance where confusion veils the air,
For truth need not in thunder be unbound,
To serve as compass in a world of care.
A single spark that holds against the gale
May prove more sure than torches vast and pale.

32

The sculptor studies marble cold and bare,
And sees within what others fail to trace,
A hidden curve, a form both strong and fair,
Awaiting patient hands to grant it face.
Each strike removes what does not yet belong,
Each measured blow refines the inward shape,
Till from the block emerges clear and strong
A figure freed from stone’s enclosing drape.
So too the self by discipline is formed,
As flaws are chipped and roughness pared away,
For through resistance character is warmed,
And purpose carved from common clay.
By patient cuts the truest lines appear,
And what was trapped stands radiant and clear.

33

When whispers drift across the midnight hall,
And ancient rafters creak in solemn tone,
The house recalls the footsteps once in thrall,
Of lives long lived and stories overthrown.
The portraits fade yet still retain a gaze,
As though their silent watch would not depart,
And shadows move in long remembered ways,
That stir the chambers of a listening heart.
So memory inhabits present space,
And lingers where affection once was laid,
It leaves upon the air a subtle trace,
Though years have dimmed what time has not delayed.
In quiet rooms where past and present meet,
The pulse of vanished days continues beat.

34

The plowman turns the furrowed field at dawn,
And cleaves the earth with steady, measured stride,
No herald greets the labor he has drawn,
Nor crowds attend to watch the soil divide.
Yet from his toil the hidden seed is cast,
And covered by the patient, yielding ground,
Though rain may fall and bitter winds blow past,
In silent dark its promise shall be found.
So work unseen may bear the richest yield,
Though applause be absent from its faithful art,
For fruit matures in humble, hidden field,
Long after idle boast has lost its start.
What patient hands commit to careful earth
Shall rise in time with undeniable worth.

35

The distant bell upon the harbor tower
Announces hours the tide cannot ignore,
Its measured toll marks fleeting human power,
Against the rhythm of the ocean’s roar.
The ships may come and go at fortune’s will,
Their sails inflated by uncertain breeze,
Yet steadfast stands the tower on the hill,
Unmoved by trade or tempests of the seas.
Thus principle must anchor wandering mind,
And hold its course when currents twist and pull,
For shifting winds may leave the soul behind,
If guided only by what seems most full.
In fixed conviction wisely held and known,
The heart withstands what change has overthrown.

36

When rivalry ignites the restless heart,
And stirs ambition’s bright but dangerous flame,
It urges men their fellows to outstart,
And measure worth by triumph and acclaim.
Yet contest need not poison noble aim,
If tempered well by fairness and respect,
For striving sharpened free from scornful blame
May lift both sides toward excellence direct.
Let victory be crowned with gracious speech,
And loss received without resentful art,
For honor stands within the way we reach
The goal that tests the sinew and the heart.
In contest shaped by virtue’s guiding hand,
Both gain a strength no gold can command.

37

The rain descends upon the city stone,
And draws from pavement scents of distant spring,
It writes in silver script on streets alone,
A transient yet expressive offering.
The hurried crowd beneath umbrellas pass,
Unmindful of the cleansing gift above,
Yet every drop upon the thirsting grass
Prepares the earth for roots of future love.
So blessings fall unnoticed in their hour,
And seem but inconvenience to the eye,
Till later blooms reveal their quiet power,
And gratitude replaces former sigh.
What first appears as burden or delay
May nourish hidden gardens on the way.

38

The minstrel tunes his lute before the crowd,
And tests each string with careful, listening ear,
He will not trust to volume bold and loud,
But seeks the note that rings both true and clear.
For harmony depends on balanced strain,
Too tight the cord may snap beneath its might,
Too loose it yields a dull, uncertain pain,
And fails to carry melody aright.
So lives must find their tension rightly set,
Between indulgence and austerity,
For either bound too firmly met
Distorts the song of full humanity.
In tempered stretch where measured forces blend,
The sweetest tones upon the air ascend.

39

The pilgrim kneels beside the winding road,
And rests his staff against the ancient stone,
His shoulders bear a long and dusty load,
Yet hope within his breast remains his own.
No promise written on the sky is shown,
Nor sudden voice descends to clear his fear,
Yet faith sustains what reason leaves alone,
And guides his feet though destination unclear.
Thus trust may flourish where no sign appears,
And strength arise without external proof,
For inward certainty through passing years
Becomes the shelter of the seeking roof.
In steadfast steps though sight be dim or far,
The heart proceeds by its enduring star.

40

When twilight yields at last to breaking dawn,
And pale horizon kindles into flame,
The night that seemed eternal now is gone,
And fear recedes that once had held its claim.
The birds resume their bright and certain song,
And dew adorns the meadow’s waiting face,
While light restores what darkness hid so long,
And breathes new vigor into silent space.
So sorrow too shall fade before the day,
However vast its shadow seemed at first,
For grief like night must yield to rising ray,
And hope returns to quench the spirit’s thirst.
No darkness holds dominion without end,
Where faithful light prepares again to ascend.

41

When silver rain upon the rooftop falls,
And murmurs low against the shuttered pane,
It threads through night in soft, unhurried calls,
And cools the fevered memory of pain.
The streets grow dim beneath its gentle sweep,
And lantern light grows blurred in amber haze,
While weary minds descend to needed sleep,
Released from daylight’s sharp and crowded maze.
So mercy comes not always crowned in might,
But sometimes clothed in quiet, steady grace,
It dims the edge of anger’s sharpened sight,
And smooths the furrowed lines upon the face.
In patient drops that ask no grand display,
The harshest heat is slowly washed away.

42

The captain stands before the rising gale,
His weathered hand upon the creaking mast,
He knows full well the canvas yet may fail,
And fragile wood may splinter in the blast.
Yet fear shall not unman his settled gaze,
Nor trembling doubt command his chosen course,
For courage grows in trial’s testing blaze,
And steadies will against opposing force.
So leadership is proved in storm, not sun,
When easy tides no skillful art require,
And honor shines in what is firmly done,
Though all around seems bent on dark desire.
The helm held fast through tempests fierce and wild
Secures safe harbor for the weakest child.

43

A single seed within the barren ground
Awaits the kiss of rain and warming light,
No sign of life upon its husk is found,
Yet hidden strength prepares for upward fight.
The soil above appears a heavy tomb,
Unyielding weight against its fragile core,
Yet from that press shall living green resume,
And split the earth that sealed it days before.
So buried hopes beneath discouragement
May gather force unseen by outward eye,
Till sudden growth in resolute ascent
Breaks through the doubt that bade ambition die.
What seems entombed in silence and despair
May rise renewed in fresher, braver air.

44

The scholar’s lamp burns late against the dark,
And scatters gold across the waiting page,
He searches truth in every subtle mark,
And weighs each word with disciplined engage.
Yet wisdom dwells not solely in the text,
Nor knowledge rest in volumes tightly bound,
For lived experience stands close annexed,
And deepens thought where ink alone is found.
Let study join with humble, patient deed,
And theory meet the tempering of life,
For seeds of thought require embodied seed,
To stand unshaken amid worldly strife.
When mind and action walk in measured pace,
Understanding finds its truest place.

45

The meadow wakes beneath the tender dawn,
And lifts its dewy lashes to the sun,
The hush of night in quiet folds withdrawn,
As golden light declares the day begun.
The lark ascends with fearless, ringing cry,
And spills its anthem over field and stream,
While shadows shrink before the warming sky,
And earth emerges from its fading dream.
So hope returns though grief has held its reign,
And lifts the heart from sorrow’s heavy shroud,
For morning writes in light across our pain,
A promise clear though yesterday was loud.
No night persists beyond appointed span,
Where patient dawn renews the strength of man.

46

The mason sets each stone with careful hand,
And checks the line against the guiding thread,
He builds not haste but structure meant to stand,
Long after fleeting praise has long since fled.
Each layer rests upon the one below,
And bears the weight of what is yet to rise,
For foundations hidden none may show
Determine if the tallest tower flies.
So character is formed in unseen hours,
In habits small and choices little praised,
For public triumph draws from secret powers,
In private disciplines securely raised.
What stands secure against the wind and rain
Was fashioned first in effort free of gain.

47

The tide retreats along the gleaming shore,
And leaves behind a map of shining sand,
The shells once tossed in ocean’s vibrant roar
Now rest in quiet reach of searching hand.
The waves return in measured, ancient art,
And claim again what briefly lay revealed,
Thus loss and gain perform their shifting part,
As fortunes rise and fall in turning field.
Let not the heart grow proud in rising swell,
Nor faint when waters seem to draw away,
For rhythms greater than our will compel
The ebb and flow that mark each mortal day.
In patient trust through change that none can bar,
We learn to stand where constant cycles are.

48

The gardener trims the branch that grows astray,
And prunes excess that chokes the tender vine,
He sacrifices bloom of present day,
To shape a harvest richer in design.
The cut may seem a loss in passing glance,
And leaves appear diminished to the eye,
Yet future fruit depends on measured chance,
And present loss prepares abundance nigh.
So discipline though sharp in moment’s sting,
Secures the strength that later shall abound,
For sacrifice is seed of better thing,
And order gives to chaos solid ground.
Through wise restraint the fullest joys are grown,
Where careless plenty leaves but waste alone.

49

When voices clash in heated, hurried debate,
And tempers flare in restless, rising tone,
The truth may flee behind the guarded gate,
Where pride erects a fortress of its own.
Yet patient listening cools the fervent air,
And opens paths where reason may be found,
For ears attentive show a deeper care,
Than louder speech that seeks to merely sound.
Let dialogue replace the sharpened blade,
And seek to mend where conflict might divide,
For understanding, gently offered aid,
Builds bridges strong where hostile walls abide.
In quiet strength that yields to wiser aim,
We win more ground than any shouted claim.

50

The traveler rests beside the winding stream,
And watches currents glide in silver thread,
He measures life not solely by the dream,
But by the steady steps his journey led.
The peaks once distant now behind him stand,
And valleys crossed lie quiet in his wake,
Each mile has shaped the firmness of his hand,
And taught his heart what burdens it can take.
So progress lives not only in the end,
But in the faithful walking of the way,
For goals achieved new distances extend,
And summon onward steps another day.
In patient stride through pathways yet untrod,
We find our strength companioned still by God.

51

When winter loosens from the stiffened ground,
And subtle warmth persuades the frost to yield,
A hidden stirring may at last be found,
Beneath the crust of long-embattled field.
The brooks resume their long-forgotten speech,
And swollen buds betray a secret rise,
While light extends its soft, persuasive reach,
And lengthens patient blue across the skies.
So hearts long bound in grief’s unyielding chain
May feel at first a scarcely sensed release,
Till hope like thawing earth absorbs the pain,
And quiet shoots announce returning peace.
What seemed entombed in cold’s unending reign
Revives when touched by mercy’s gentle rain.

52

The tower clock proclaims the passing hour,
Indifferent to joy or hidden tears,
Its iron tongue asserts a measured power,
Unmoved by mortal hopes or mortal fears.
The seconds fall like grains of sifted sand,
And none may halt their downward, silent glide,
No plea can stay the clock’s impartial hand,
Nor bargain gain what slipping time denied.
Then let each hour be stewarded with care,
And filled with labors worthy of its span,
For wasted minutes thin the richest share,
And rob tomorrow’s promise from the plan.
In conscious use of moments quickly flown,
We fashion futures firmly as our own.

53

The vineyard bends beneath September’s weight,
And heavy clusters gleam in purple shade,
The patient vigneron attends the gate,
And counts the fruit by careful seasons made.
No single day the harvest can secure,
Nor sudden heat compel the grape to sweet,
For sweetness ripens only when endure
The measured sun and cool of night complete.
So virtue matures through lengthened years,
And cannot bloom by haste of shallow will,
For trials weather hopes and temper fears,
Till strength is formed in habits practiced still.
What time refines in steady, quiet art
Becomes the wine that cheers the faithful heart.

54

The river mirrors heaven’s drifting light,
And bends in silver curves through valley floor,
It yields to stone yet keeps its onward flight,
And finds through yielding strength a greater shore.
No rigid cliff can wholly bar its flow,
For patient water carves the stubborn land,
And subtle pressure laid by constant throw
Reshapes what iron force could not command.
Thus gentleness may prove the firmer might,
Where anger breaks against resistant wall,
For quiet persistence in the right
Outlasts the clash that seeks to conquer all.
By steady course through obstacles arrayed,
The softest stream can cut the deepest glade.

55

A letter sealed and carried far from home,
Contains within its fold a beating heart,
Across wide seas and miles of salted foam
It bridges souls that distance would apart.
The ink though faint conveys a living breath,
And speaks what trembling lips might never dare,
It stands against forgetfulness and death,
And plants remembrance gently in the air.
So written words may outlast mortal voice,
And bind two minds though years between them lie,
For language gives to love a lasting choice,
And lifts affection where goodbyes would sigh.
In simple lines by faithful spirit penned,
The absent friend remains a present friend.

56

The mountain path grows narrow near the height,
And thinner air makes labored every breath,
Yet upward still the climber fixes sight,
Undaunted by the valley’s distant depth.
The summit gleams though clouds obscure the view,
And doubt suggests retreat to safer ground,
But courage answers faint though firm and true,
And bids the wavering heart in hope abound.
For peaks are reached by steady, stepwise gain,
Not leaps of reckless pride in haste begun,
And toil that presses through the strain
Rewards the one whose will is not undone.
Where others turn for comfort of the plain,
The steadfast find the vista worth the pain.

57

The scholar listens more than he proclaims,
And gathers wisdom from the humblest voice,
He tempers judgment, cautious in his claims,
And weighs each path before he makes his choice.
He knows that truth is rarely wholly won
By single glance or hasty, eager thought,
But formed where many tested strands are spun,
And insight through reflection slowly wrought.
Thus understanding grows from patient ear,
And ripens in the soil of open mind,
For pride that claims all answers crystal clear
Leaves deeper, hidden questions still behind.
In quiet study joined with gracious grace,
True knowledge finds a balanced dwelling place.

58

The garden wall in ivy wrapped and green,
Appears to guard the blossoms safe within,
Yet vines unchecked may choke the tender scene,
And rob the roots of light they might have been.
So boundaries must be wisely shaped and kept,
Not raised so high they bar the needed sun,
Nor left so low that wild disorder crept,
And careful cultivation come undone.
In measured limits freedom learns to thrive,
And flourish most where structure lends its aid,
For discipline helps fragile dreams survive,
And shields young growth from being overlaid.
Where order stands with gentle, guiding art,
The fairest blooms arise in sheltered heart.

59

When grief first falls it strikes with blinding force,
And darkens sight with overwhelming shade,
It seems to halt the mind in fixed remorse,
And steal the strength by which resolve was made.
Yet sorrow softens under passing days,
And memory reshapes its bitter sting,
Till loss becomes a quieter, deeper phrase,
And yields a wisdom suffering may bring.
For pain though sharp in its immediate hour,
May plant compassion in the tender breast,
And grant to fragile hearts a gentler power,
To comfort others similarly pressed.
Thus tears once shed in anguish and in fear
May water seeds of kindness year by year.

60

The compass needle trembles yet remains,
Aligned though storms disturb the vessel’s frame,
It shifts but never from its north refrains,
Nor yields its core to shifting winds of flame.
So conscience guides amid confusion’s sea,
And points toward good though tempests cloud the chart,
It may be tested by adversity,
Yet holds its quiet center in the heart.
When voices clamor loud with tempting plea,
And shortcuts promise swift and easy gain,
Let inward truth define what ought to be,
And steer the will through profit’s glittered lane.
For steadfast aim by principle made clear
Secures the course when choices disappear.

61

When silence settles on the crowded square,
And merchants fold their banners for the night,
A gentler rhythm softens in the air,
And lanterns bloom in halos warm and bright.
The stones that bore the thunder of the day
Now cool beneath the hush of drifting stars,
And echoes of contention fade away,
Like distant chords released from iron bars.
So in retreat from clamor’s pressing call
The mind regains its measured, steady tone,
And finds within the quiet of it all
A deeper strength than noise has ever shown.
In chosen stillness wisdom learns to speak,
And fortifies the heart grown faint and weak.

62

The sailor scans the ever-shifting chart,
Where currents twist beyond the naked eye,
He reads the sky as one who knows by heart
The coded hints that clouds may signify.
No map can wholly bind the restless sea,
Nor rule confine the wind’s uncertain art,
Yet skill and watchful patience help him see
The wiser course that keeps the vessel’s part.
So foresight joins with courage in the test,
And neither stands alone against the tide,
For boldness blind will seldom fare its best,
Nor caution cold secure a faithful guide.
When prudence and resolve together steer,
The roughest waters grow less dark with fear.

63

A worn-out path across the meadow plain
Reveals where countless feet have passed before,
Each step has left a trace in sun or rain,
And shaped the grass along a silent lore.
The way seems easier for those who tread
Where others bore the burden first in line,
Yet someone once through unmarked grasses led,
And carved the route by effort’s firm design.
So pioneers in quiet courage go,
Where none have walked and no assurance lies,
They bear the doubt that future men may know
A clearer course beneath familiar skies.
What now is road was once uncertain field,
And progress springs from those who would not yield.

64

The candle flickers though the room be still,
And casts its shadow long against the wall,
Its fragile flame obeys a hidden will,
Yet stands defiant lest the darkness fall.
It spends itself in steady, measured glow,
And offers light though wax be slowly lost,
No tally kept of all it must forego,
Nor fear confessed of sacrificial cost.
So generosity in humble guise
Illumes the paths where others seek their way,
And though its gift before the daylight dies,
Its warmth remains when flame has passed away.
In quiet giving lies a power unseen,
That brightens worlds without a boasting sheen.

65

The farmer counts the clouds along the ridge,
And weighs the promise held in distant gray,
He knows that rain may flood the lowest bridge,
Or pass his fields and wander far astray.
Yet still he plants in hope’s persistent trust,
And tends the soil with patient, watchful care,
For harvest seldom rises from the dust,
Unless a faithful hand has labored there.
Thus effort must precede the blessing sought,
Though outcome rest beyond the mortal hand,
For seed uncast yields nothing dearly bought,
And idle fields no golden sheaves command.
In active hope where diligence is sown,
The fruits of grace more surely may be grown.

66

The poet walks beneath the moonlit dome,
And listens for the cadence of the night,
He gathers whispers drifting far from home,
And shapes them into verse of measured light.
No coin compels the birth of honest song,
Nor praise alone sustains the lyric flame,
For truth once felt must find its form ere long,
And language serve what stirred before it came.
So art is born where inward stirring calls,
And not where fashion dictates passing theme,
It rises like a fountain through the walls
Of doubt, and flows from deeper, truer stream.
When heart and word in faithful union blend,
The lines endure beyond their mortal end.

67

A bridge extends across the rushing span,
And binds two shores once severed by the stream,
It stands because a thoughtful, patient plan
Has joined strong arch with firm and steady beam.
No single stone could claim the crossing whole,
Yet each must bear its portion of the strain,
For unity is built by ordered role,
And strength is shared in mutual domain.
So communities like bridges rise,
When varied gifts in harmony are placed,
And none their neighbor’s needed skill despise,
Nor seek alone the central, honored space.
In woven effort spanning depth and divide,
A greater whole is formed on either side.

68

The orchard blooms though winter once held sway,
And blossoms white adorn the branching tree,
What seemed but lifeless wood in cold decay
Now hums with bees in vibrant industry.
So change may mask itself in barren guise,
And seasons shift where none expect a turn,
For cycles hidden from impatient eyes
Prepare the growth for which we later yearn.
Let not despair conclude the present scene,
Nor judge too soon what time has yet to prove,
For hidden springs may feed the unseen green,
And silent roots in secret strength improve.
What appears ended in a frosted frame
May rise renewed in altered, brighter flame.

69

The judge who sits with measured, tempered gaze
Must weigh not only statute but the heart,
For rigid codes in unexamined ways
May wound where mercy ought to play its part.
Yet law abandoned wholly to desire
Would drift like ship without a guiding star,
And justice falter, bent by passing fire,
Till fairness fades in arbitrary war.
Thus balance must in careful wisdom stand,
Where truth and grace together hold the scale,
And neither rule with isolated hand,
Lest either sternness or indulgence fail.
In tempered rule where equity is found,
A steadier peace within the realm abounds.

70

The traveler nearing twilight of his road
Looks back upon the miles his feet have known,
Each burden borne, each lighter, shared load,
Has shaped the strength that now is fully grown.
He sees not only peaks once hard to climb,
But valleys crossed with trembling, doubtful tread,
And learns that faithful walking over time
Has given substance to the words he said.
So aging is not merely loss of bloom,
But ripening of insight patiently earned,
For years though marking passage toward the tomb
Also reveal what youthful haste had spurned.
In gathered light of days both bright and dim,
A richer song remains at journey’s brim.

71

When evening spills her copper into blue,
And quiet gathers softly on the land,
The day withdraws with one last burning hue,
Like ember cupped within a cooling hand.
The swallows carve brief signatures on air,
Then vanish where the dim horizon lies,
While fields release a slow, contented prayer
That drifts unseen beneath the darkening skies.
So labors end though effort marked the hours,
And rest descends where strain had firmly stood,
For even strength must yield to gentler powers,
And find renewal in the calm of good.
In measured dusk the weary spirit knows
That fruitful toil requires composed repose.

72

The smith who bends above the heated steel
Strikes not at random but with tempered aim,
Each blow controlled by knowledge hands can feel,
Each spark a fleeting witness to the flame.
The iron softens under ordered force,
And takes the shape imagination planned,
Yet chaos would distort its destined course
If haste should overrule the guiding hand.
Thus discipline refines the fervent will,
And channels passion toward enduring form,
For energy unguided may do ill,
Though born in heat as bright as any storm.
When fire and wisdom labor side by side,
A stronger blade emerges from the tide.

73

The orchard path in early spring is bare,
Yet subtle signs foretell the coming bloom,
A fragrance faint upon the thawing air
Hints at the life concealed in winter’s gloom.
The branches seem but rigid lines of gray,
And brittle bark reveals no tender hue,
Yet sap ascends in secret, quiet way,
Preparing buds to split the sky in two.
So progress often moves beyond the sight
Of those who judge by present outward frame,
For growth begins in hidden depth and might
Before it dares to speak its future name.
Let patience guard the interval unseen,
For roots are working where no leaves have been.

74

The watchman stands where city walls grow high,
And keeps his vigil through the drifting night,
No trumpet sounds beneath the moonlit sky,
No crowd applauds his steadfast, silent sight.
He marks the shifting shadow on the stone,
And notes the flicker far beyond the gate,
For threats are seldom loudly overthrown,
But creep where men grow careless at their state.
So conscience guards the citadel within,
And scans the thoughts that wander near the line,
It warns before temptation turns to sin,
And steadies will before desire incline.
In faithful watch against the subtle fall,
The heart preserves its quiet, inner wall.

75

A traveler rests beside the winding stream,
And cups cool water in his calloused hand,
The mirrored sky within the current’s gleam
Reflects a world he cannot quite command.
The ripples blur the image into light,
And yet the substance of the sky remains,
Though surface shifts beneath the day and night,
The deeper flow unbroken still sustains.
Thus circumstance may alter outward face,
And fortunes rise and sink like passing foam,
Yet deeper truths abide in hidden place,
And grant the wandering spirit steadier home.
In shifting scenes where outward forms depart,
The truest anchors dwell within the heart.

76

The quill that scratches softly on the page
Leaves slender marks that outlive mortal breath,
It captures thought unbound by passing age,
And grants a fragile mind reprieve from death.
The ink though dark conveys a shining flame,
A spark of self preserved in ordered line,
And words once whispered quietly in name
Find permanence in script’s enduring sign.
So writing binds the present to the past,
And builds a bridge across the silent years,
For fleeting voices seldom ever last,
But written truth survives beyond our fears.
In careful strokes that fix what time would move,
The mortal hand achieves a longer groove.

77

The falcon circles high above the plain,
Its steady wings outspread in measured glide,
It rides the currents shaped by sun and rain,
And trusts the unseen lifts the winds provide.
It does not thrash against the flowing air,
Nor waste its strength in aimless, frantic beat,
But leans into the sky with patient care,
And finds through yielding flight both strength and seat.
So wisdom learns the patterns of the hour,
And works with forces larger than its will,
For struggle blind consumes the spirit’s power,
While insight bends and yet remains upright still.
In harmony with tides no man commands,
The soul finds strength in larger, guiding hands.

78

The judge of self is often swift and stern,
And weighs each flaw with unforgiving scale,
Yet mercy is a lesson we must learn,
Lest harshness cause the inner hope to fail.
For growth requires allowance for the fall,
And strength emerges from corrected course,
No child stands firm without first learning small,
Nor courage blooms without some trembling source.
Thus grace must temper justice in the mind,
And patience soften self-imposed decree,
For in compassion we more clearly find
The path that leads to fuller dignity.
When inward law is balanced well with grace,
The spirit rises steadier in its place.

79

The lighthouse stands against the crashing foam,
And sends its beam across the blackened tide,
A solitary guardian of home,
Whose steady arc no darkness can divide.
The sailors scanning through the salted spray
Discern its signal through the swirling rain,
And though the tempest seeks to block their way,
Its patient pulse assures them not in vain.
So guidance offered constant, clear, and true
May pierce the storms that cloud another’s sight,
For faithful presence helps the weak renew
Their courage in the most uncertain night.
In steadfast light that does not cease to burn,
Lost vessels find the harbor for return.

80

When twilight’s hush descends on golden grain,
And harvest stands in patient, bending rows,
The farmer walks the length of all his pain,
And measures what the changing season owes.
Each furrow carved beneath the summer sun,
Each seed once buried in uncertain trust,
Now speaks of labor faithfully begun,
And fields once bare transformed from silent dust.
Thus perseverance yields its quiet wage,
Though no swift miracle secured the gain,
For faithful effort through the turning age
Has shaped abundance from enduring strain.
In final sheaves where scattered seeds once lay,
The long, unseen devotion finds its day.

81

When morning breaks across the quiet hill,
And gilds the mist that lingers in the vale,
The waking earth resumes its patient will,
Unfurling light like some translucent sail.
The cattle stir, the lark begins its cry,
And furrows shine where dew has laid its claim,
While distant roofs beneath the warming sky
Awake to life without a trumpet’s flame.
So hope returns without a sounding bell,
And grows in silence where the heart had feared,
For dawn performs what darkness could not quell,
And brings to sight what night obscured and veered.
In gentle rise of unannounced new day,
Despair retreats and strength resumes its sway.

82

The scholar turns a brittle, yellowed page,
And finds within its ink a living voice,
Though centuries have marked its outer age,
The thought endures, compelling present choice.
No mortal frame can outlast passing years,
Yet minds once stirred may echo still in mind,
For written truth outlives the mortal fears,
And bridges gaps no distance could unwind.
So let our labors aim beyond the hour,
And shape ideas worthy to remain,
For seeds of thought possess enduring power,
To bloom in fields we shall not see again.
In words made firm and faithful to the core,
We build foundations for a distant shore.

83

The thunder cracks across the startled plain,
And splits the quiet of the waiting air,
The lightning’s flash reveals in moment’s reign
What darkness long had hidden unaware.
Yet storm though fierce cannot forever stand,
Its rage exhausts the clouds from which it grew,
And rain descends to soften hardened land,
Till gentler skies emerge in fresher hue.
Thus anger flares in heat of wounded pride,
But loses strength when reason cools the flame,
And hearts once closed by fury’s swelling tide
May yield to calmer thought and tempered aim.
No tempest holds dominion without cease,
Where patience cultivates returning peace.

84

The bridge of stone across the river laid
Unites two realms once parted by the stream,
Its arches cast a patient, cooling shade,
And bind what once was split by rushing gleam.
No single block could span the churning tide,
Nor isolated strength secure the frame,
But joined in ordered purpose side by side,
The stones achieve what none alone could claim.
So partnership in mutual design
Accomplishes what solitude cannot,
For shared endeavor knits each separate line,
And lifts combined success from humble plot.
In balanced strength where many efforts blend,
A wider path toward greater ends we wend.

85

The winter sun though pale upon the snow
Still casts a brilliance clean upon the field,
Its modest rays in quiet manner glow,
And prove that light need not be fierce to yield.
The branches etched against the silver sky
Stand stark yet beautiful in frozen art,
And life beneath the surface does not die,
Though outward signs from warmth appear apart.
So influence may dwell in subtle grace,
And shape the world without a boastful claim,
For steady warmth in unassuming place
May outlast flashes of a louder flame.
In tempered light that neither blinds nor fades,
A lasting strength through patient hours pervades.

86

The merchant counts his ledgers late at night,
And tallies gain against invested cost,
He measures risk by figures plain and tight,
And weighs what might be earned or might be lost.
Yet wealth confined to coin and minted store
Cannot secure the heart from inward lack,
For gold may heap upon the counting floor
While joy slips silent through an unseen crack.
Thus profit must be balanced by the good,
And commerce serve a larger, human aim,
For fortune divorced from brotherhood
Leaves hollow triumph masked by glittered name.
In generous use of what abundance brings,
True richness flowers beyond material things.

87

The mason carves his mark upon the stone,
A modest sign of labor firmly done,
No grand inscription claiming deeds alone,
But proof that careful craftsmanship was won.
The wall may stand beyond his mortal span,
And weather storms he never lived to see,
Yet hidden in its strength remains the plan
Of hands that shaped its quiet integrity.
So character is built in daily choice,
In acts unseen by fame’s discerning eye,
For inner form gives outer works their voice,
And silent virtues never wholly die.
In stones of habit laid with faithful care,
A life becomes a fortress strong and fair.

88

The harvest moon ascends in amber glow,
And bathes the stubbled fields in tempered light,
The labored acres resting now below
Reflect a season’s toil in softened sight.
The barns stand filled where once were empty rows,
And grain lies stored against the coming cold,
For foresight planted seeds that patience chose,
And steady hands their quiet promise hold.
Thus preparation guards against surprise,
And planning tempers fortune’s sudden turn,
For wisdom sees beyond immediate eyes,
And stores what future needs may later earn.
In careful thought that spans beyond the now,
Security is shaped by mindful vow.

89

The wanderer who walks an unmarked road
Relies not solely on familiar sign,
But trusts the inward compass he has sowed,
And reads the land for subtle, hidden line.
No milestone stands to mark each turning bend,
Nor map disclose what lies beyond the crest,
Yet courage acts though knowledge cannot mend
The veil that hides what future may suggest.
So faith proceeds where certainty grows thin,
And steps are taken without full design,
For every path begins in choice within,
Before it shows its outline clear and fine.
In daring stride where doubt would counsel stay,
New landscapes open to the willing way.

90

When twilight’s final embers fade to gray,
And constellations stitch the velvet dome,
The earth lies hushed at ending of the day,
Awaiting rest within its circling home.
The hush is not of emptiness but peace,
A pause between the labors yet to rise,
Where restless thoughts and anxious motions cease,
And calm descends like balm on weary eyes.
So endings are not merely loss of light,
But thresholds where renewal may begin,
For night prepares the ground for morning bright,
And quiet cleanses noise that clung within.
In cycles traced by shadow, star, and sun,
Completion whispers what new starts have won.

91

When morning frost adorns the silent field,
And breath hangs pale in air not yet made warm,
The earth beneath its crystal veil concealed
Awaits the gentle sun’s restoring form.
The rigid grass beneath that shining lace
Seems lifeless in the brittle winter gleam,
Yet warmth will soon its hidden pulse embrace,
And wake the roots from out their frozen dream.
So hearts encased in seasons cold and stark
May still contain a living, quiet spark,
Though outward signs suggest a settled dark,
And hope appears a faint, receding mark.
For under ice where silent currents run,
Life stirs unseen toward spring and rising sun.

92

The builder marks his measure on the beam,
And checks each angle with a steady eye,
He will not trust to guesswork or to dream,
But lays each joint with care that time defy.
No single error left unheeded long
But weakens all the structure yet to rise,
For strength is born where patient lines are strong,
And careful craft rejects what compromise.
So habits small and choices daily made
Shape character more firm than fleeting show,
For virtue rests in patterns well obeyed,
And not in vows proclaimed with fervent glow.
In faithful detail, rightly placed and done,
A life is built that storms cannot outrun.

93

The distant star though faint against the night
Still holds its ancient station in the sky,
It shines beyond the range of mortal sight
Long after watchers close their weary eye.
Its light began its journey years ago,
And only now arrives to waiting earth,
A silent witness to the cosmic flow
Of time that measures distance, depth, and worth.
So influence may travel far and slow,
And touch a soul unseen by present gaze,
For seeds of kindness we in darkness sow
May bloom in fields beyond our numbered days.
In patient arc where unseen forces move,
Our smallest acts their lasting power prove.

94

The harvest cart rolls slowly through the lane,
Its wooden wheels in steady cadence turn,
The gathered grain once scattered in the rain
Now rests in sheaves the waiting barns discern.
Each seed that met the soil in hopeful trust
Has answered toil with golden, bending yield,
What once lay hidden in unmarked dust
Now crowns the labor of the tended field.
Thus effort joined with seasons rightly kept
Produces fruit beyond the sower’s sight,
For what in faith was quietly swept
Into the earth returns in fuller light.
In cycles traced by patience and by care,
Abundance grows from hands that chose to bear.

95

The mountain brook descends in silver thread,
And sings against the stones in lively tune,
It neither fears the drop that lies ahead
Nor mourns the loss of height it knew at noon.
It moves because its nature is to flow,
And finds in downward course a wider plain,
For rivers broaden as they onward go,
And gather strength from tributary rain.
So change though seen as loss of former height
May lead to broader fields and deeper ways,
For wisdom grows when guided into light,
And life expands beyond its youthful blaze.
In letting go what once seemed all we knew,
We find a truer, larger vantage view.

96

The watchful gardener in autumn mild
Collects the seeds from summer’s fading bloom,
He knows that what appears now spent and wild
Contains the promise of another plume.
He dries and stores what petals once displayed,
And guards them through the lean and barren cold,
For future gardens quietly are made
From remnants of the season past and old.
So endings hide beginnings in their frame,
And loss may serve as keeper of the new,
For cycles shift though essence stays the same,
And faded hues prepare for fresher hue.
In careful tending of what once has been,
Tomorrow’s flowers rise from stored within.

97

The marketplace resounds with eager cry,
And voices bargain through the heated air,
Yet honest trade must lift its standard high,
And temper gain with equitable care.
For wealth amassed by cunning, sharp deceit
May glitter bright yet hollow be its core,
While fairness builds a foundation meet
That prospers long when greed can thrive no more.
So conscience in the counting must remain,
And justice shape the scale on either side,
For profit severed from the common gain
Leaves fractures wide no ledger can divide.
In commerce ruled by honor’s steady hand,
True prosperity strengthens all the land.

98

The scholar bends before the ancient tome,
And traces thoughts in careful, quiet line,
He knows that wisdom is not born at home,
But gathered where past voices intertwine.
Each page a bridge from ages long ago
Extends its span to present, questioning mind,
For learning grows when humble hearts will bow,
And seek in former paths what truth they find.
Thus tradition and fresh insight may
Converse in fruitful, thoughtful harmony,
Where reverence does not bar a newer way,
Nor novelty reject old constancy.
In dialogue where time and thought agree,
A fuller vision comes to clearer see.

99

The traveler nearing sunset of his road
Reflects on miles beneath his steady tread,
Each burden borne, each lighter, shared load
Has shaped the strength by which he now is led.
He sees the valleys once with doubt approached,
And heights once feared now resting far behind,
And learns that faith through darkest hours encroached
Has carved resilience deep within the mind.
So age reveals not only lines of care,
But harvest gathered from enduring days,
For wisdom ripens in the seasoned air,
And tempers flame that once in haste would blaze.
In twilight glow of journey almost done,
A deeper light than youth’s bright flare is won.

100

When night descends in velvet, starry sweep,
And constellations crown the silent dome,
The earth in turning vigil seems to keep
A rhythm older than its fleeting home.
No mortal plan directs that cosmic dance,
Nor human will commands the wheeling spheres,
Yet in that vast and ordered circumstance
Our fleeting lives find meaning through the years.
So humble hearts beneath expansive sky
May sense their place within a grand design,
For though we live and labor, love and die,
Our stories join a pattern more divine.
In quiet awe before the heavens spread,
The soul finds rest where larger truths are read.

101

When twilight pools in amber on the lake,
And mirrored hills grow soft in fading light,
The ripples calm for evening’s quiet sake,
And day withdraws into the arms of night.
The reeds stand still as if in whispered prayer,
And distant bells drift faint across the shore,
While cooling air moves gently through the air,
And labor’s restless clamor is no more.
So peace descends where once was anxious strain,
And quiet thought resumes its tempered pace,
For silence after tumult and after pain
Restores the mind to steadier grace.
In hushed reflection at the day’s slow end,
The spirit gathers strength again to mend.

102

The smithy glows beneath the midnight spark,
